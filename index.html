The one filming rule that makes these videos work

Stop talking while you operate. Show the action and let the mechanical sounds carry it — the ramp locking in, the ratchet clicking, the frame folding shut. Record those clean, and save voiceover for before or after the action, not on top of it. Show, don't narrate.
